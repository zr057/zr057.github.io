PHEV Energy and Thermal Management

A coordinated simulation framework for comparing online and globally optimized energy-management strategies under the CLTC-P driving cycle.

Project scope

  • Built a longitudinal power-demand model for the CLTC-P driving cycle.
  • Modeled fuel consumption, motor efficiency, SOC, and first-order battery thermal dynamics.
  • Implemented CD-CS, ECMS, and thermal-constrained ECMS.
  • Used dynamic programming as a global optimization benchmark.
  • Evaluated fuel use, electrical energy, terminal SOC, peak temperature, and temperature-limit violations.

Methods

Technical stack: Python, NumPy, pandas, SciPy, and Matplotlib.
